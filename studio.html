Ngoma · AI Video Editor

Ngoma, the talking drum, carries your message: raw video in; designed clips, captions and the post out.

For: podcasters, coaches and teams turning interviews, talks and podcasts into content.

  • Tell Ngoma what you want, in your own words
  • Long pauses, "um"s and stutters cut out of the full episode
  • The best moments as 9:16 clips: modern captions, emojis, icons, counting numbers, key-point cards and your own Canva stickers
  • Your logo, intro and end cards; the title, description, hashtags and chapters

You provide: a raw video and one sentence of instructions.

Software delivers: MP4s ready to post, edited on your own computer. The video is never uploaded.
