About

Otieno Thomas

I work at the intersection of strategy, data, and delivery — three things most consultants split across three different people. I don't split them.

Most communication advice is generic because it skips the first question: what does the data actually say about this audience, this market, this moment? I start there, then build the message, then coach the delivery — same person, same process, from the research behind your positioning through to the room where you actually say it.

I work across founders, NGO leaders, and corporate teams in Uganda and East Africa, blending global strategy method with the cultural fluency that makes a message actually land here — not a translated version of a Silicon Valley playbook.
