About

Otieno Thomas

I work at the intersection of strategy, data, delivery — and the software that carries them. Four things most consultants split across four different people. I don't split them.

Most communication advice is generic because it skips the first question: what does the data actually say about this audience, this market, this moment? I start there, then build the message, then coach the delivery — same person, same process, from the research behind your positioning through to the room where you actually say it.

And when the strategy needs something built to run on — a website, an application, an internal tool — I build that too. Not a referral to a developer who never sat in the positioning session. The same person, end to end.

I work across founders, NGO leaders, and corporate teams in Uganda and East Africa, blending global strategy method with the cultural fluency that makes a message actually land here — not a translated version of a Silicon Valley playbook.

How I work

Four commitments, not a slogan.

Data before narrative

The story gets built around what the evidence actually shows, not around what sounds compelling in the room.

Delivery is part of the strategy

A positioning document nobody can say out loud under pressure was never finished. I stay through the delivery, not just the deck.

The system is part of the deliverable

Positioning that lives only in a slide deck decays. If your strategy needs a website, an app, or an internal tool to stay in daily use, that gets built — not recommended.

Local fluency, not local flavor

Cultural grounding isn't a proverb dropped into a slide. It's understanding what actually moves a specific audience, in this market.
