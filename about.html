Founder · SpeakPower

Otieno Thomas

I work where communication, branding, AI and digital execution meet. I started SpeakPower around a simple belief: a strong idea is not enough if people cannot understand it, repeat it or act on it.

My approach begins with the audience and the context. What do people already believe? What are they struggling to understand? What evidence should shape the message? Then comes the narrative, the delivery and — where useful — the digital system that keeps the strategy alive.

I am especially interested in African communication that is globally credible without becoming culturally generic. The goal is not to copy a Silicon Valley playbook into Kampala. It is to build communication that works in real African rooms and can still travel beyond them.

How I work

Four commitments behind every engagement.

Audience before ego

Start with what the audience needs to understand, not what the communicator wants to say.

Evidence before polish

Use audience, market and programme evidence to shape the message before making it sound impressive.

Strategy through execution

Stay close to delivery so the positioning survives the pitch, workshop, website or internal workflow.

Local fluency, global standard

Use African context as a source of insight and precision, not as decoration or a stereotype.

What SpeakPower is becoming

A communication company with an intelligence layer.

Communication strategy is the core. AI and digital systems are the tools that make the strategy easier to repeat, scale and measure. That is the direction behind the work.
