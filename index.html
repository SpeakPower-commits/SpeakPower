POLSSE™ · SpeakPower framework

Five lenses to pressure-test a message before it travels.

Good communication is not only about words. POLSSE asks what changes when a message enters public life, an organization, a regulated environment, a security-sensitive context or people's everyday reality.

  1. P

    Politics

    Who has influence, who may challenge the message, and how might it be read beyond the room?

  2. O

    Organizations

    Can the people inside repeat one clear meaning across leadership, teams and channels?

  3. L

    Law

    Are the claims, promises and disclosures ethical, defensible and appropriate?

  4. S

    Security

    What happens to the message and the reputation once it is copied, forwarded or taken out of context?

  5. S

    Socioeconomics

    Does it make sense against people's real incentives, constraints and economic choices?
