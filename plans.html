Questions

How paying works.

Does a plan renew by itself?

No. Mobile money asks for your PIN every time, so a plan never takes money on its own. Three days before the end we remind you; renewing is one tap, and renewing early adds 30 days after your current end date, so no day is lost.

Can I switch from Starter to Pro?

Yes, any time. Pro starts the moment you pay, and your Starter time ends that day.

What happens when my 30 days end?

Nothing is lost. GRIOT keeps everything it remembers about you, and your rehearsal history stays. Renew whenever you are ready to carry on.

Can I buy a Studio pack without a membership?

Yes. Each pack has one price and is paid once, by mobile money or card; no membership is needed. With Pro, one pack a month is included and the rest are 15% off.

How do I pay?

By MTN MoMo, Airtel Money or card, through Flutterwave's secure checkout. You come straight back to SpeakPower and your plan is on at once.

Is there a limit?

Each plan has a fair-use ceiling, listed in the plan details above. In the app you see how much of your month is used, never a meter per question. A question GRIOT could not answer, a rehearsal we could not hear, or a video edit we could not hear, never counts.
