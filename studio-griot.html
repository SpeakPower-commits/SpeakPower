SpeakPower Studio · GRIOT · Early access

A strategic analyst that remembers your business.

Most AI tools meet you as a stranger every morning. You re-explain the business, the market, the constraint you are working around — and then you get a confident answer with no way to tell which half of it was invented. GRIOT is built the other way round.

The difference

It keeps context, and it shows its evidence.

GRIOT holds what it learns about your organisation and brings it back the next time you ask something. And it marks every claim it makes, so you can see what rests on something you told it and what is the model reasoning ahead of the facts.

Memory

It does not start from zero

Each conversation adds to a durable record for your organisation — the constraints, the decisions, the things that did not work. The next question starts from there.

Why it matters: the expensive part of advice is context. Paying to rebuild it every session is why most AI tools stay a novelty.

Evidence

Every claim carries its status

Answers separate fact from inference, hypothesis, recommendation and unknown. You see the reasoning, not just the conclusion.

Why it matters: a board paper built on an unmarked guess is worse than no paper. This makes the guess visible before it reaches the room.

Routing

The right lens, not one generic voice

A pricing question, a positioning question and a hiring question are not the same problem. GRIOT routes each to specialist analysis — brand, market, data, growth, operations, research — and says which it used.

Why it matters: you can tell whether it actually understood the question you asked.

Protocol

A method, run the same way every time

Understand, context, evidence, diagnose, options, recommend, execute, measure, learn. Nine steps, applied consistently rather than improvised per question.

Why it matters: consistency is what makes the output comparable across months, and what makes a decision log worth keeping.

Who it is for

Founders and directors making decisions without an analyst.

If you are the person who has to decide where the next quarter goes — and there is no strategy team between you and that call — GRIOT is the second opinion that already knows your situation.

FoundersRaising, pricing, positioning, or choosing what not to build.
NGO directorsConcept notes, donor narrative, programme trade-offs.
Comms leadsInherited a message that is not landing and need to know why.
OperatorsRunning the business and the strategy with the same pair of hands.
