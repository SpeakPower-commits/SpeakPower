How it works

Ninety seconds from nerves to a plan.

  1. Pick the moment

    Investor pitch, donor presentation, interview answer, a 60-second introduction or a toast.

  2. Speak into your phone

    Up to three minutes. The audio is scored, then discarded. Only your results are kept.

  3. Get your score and a better version

What the Speak Score measures

Exact on delivery. Honest on message.

Delivery is measured from your recording, to the word: pace (25%), fillers (20%) and long pauses (15%). The message (40%) is judged through SpeakPower's POLSSE lenses: Politics, Organizations, Law, Security and Socioeconomics. Targets: 130 to 160 words a minute, no more than 3 fillers and 1 long pause a minute.

Questions

Before you press record.

Is my recording stored?

No. The audio is turned into text, scored, and then discarded. Only your scores and the written coaching are kept, so you can see your progress, and one button deletes them.

What does it cost?

Your first 3 tries are free, shared with every SpeakPower service. After that a rehearsal costs UGX 5,000 from your balance, topped up by MTN MoMo, Airtel Money or card. If the written coaching is unavailable you get the scores for UGX 2,500, and a recording we cannot hear is never charged.

What is a good Speak Score?

Above 80 is ready for the room. Most first rehearsals land between 55 and 70; the fastest gains come from moving your ask earlier and cutting fillers. The score adds pace, fillers and pauses (60%) to how well the message holds up through SpeakPower's POLSSE lenses (40%).

Which languages and phones does it work with?

It listens for English, including East African accents. It works in Chrome on Android and Safari on iPhone, and in any modern browser on a laptop, with no app to install.
