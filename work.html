Tonninyira

Commerce platform

A browser-first commerce system designed to help businesses present products, support customer ordering and connect digital transactions to operational workflows.

Business problem

Small businesses need a practical digital storefront and order workflow without the complexity of a large enterprise commerce stack.

What was built

  • Customer-facing product discovery and filtering
  • Order and checkout flow
  • Payment integration layer
  • Operational data and backend workflows

Technology

  • HTML / CSS / JavaScript
  • Supabase
  • Postgres
  • Flutterwave
  • GitHub

Evidence

Active build and ongoing product development. Verified customer, revenue and conversion figures are intentionally not published until documented.

CuePointe

Community platform

A pool tournament and league concept designed around participation, event management, talent discovery, player records and stronger visibility for cuesports.

The opportunity

Pool talent exists across venues and communities, but participation and achievement can be difficult to track consistently across events.

What was designed

  • Player and event records
  • Match and tournament workflows
  • Talent metrics and recognition
  • Finance, sponsor and operations structures

Technology

  • Web platform
  • Structured data workflows
  • Event management
  • Digital records

Evidence

The platform is being developed as a community-driven system. Public outcome metrics will be added as they become verifiable across events.

Proof of execution

The product is part of the positioning.

These are not concept decks handed to somebody else to build. SpeakPower uses product work to test whether a message can survive contact with customers, users, operational constraints and the realities of deployment.

Positioning

Clarify who the product is for, why it matters and how it should be understood.

Product thinking

Turn the positioning into information architecture, flows, records and interfaces people can actually use.

Digital execution

Build, deploy and refine the system rather than stopping at a strategic recommendation.

Evidence standard

No inflated case-study numbers.

When verified client or product outcomes are available, each case will expand to Problem → Strategy → Build → Outcome. Until then, the site distinguishes clearly between what exists, what is being developed and what has been measured.
