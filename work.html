Tonninyira

Commerce platform

A browser-first commerce system designed to help businesses present products, support customer ordering and connect digital transactions to operational workflows.

Business problem

Small businesses need a practical digital storefront and order workflow without the complexity of a large enterprise commerce stack.

What was built

  • Customer-facing product discovery and filtering
  • Order and checkout flow
  • Payment integration layer
  • Operational data and backend workflows

Technology

  • HTML / CSS / JavaScript
  • Supabase
  • Postgres
  • Flutterwave
  • GitHub

Evidence

Active build and ongoing product development. Verified customer, revenue and conversion figures are intentionally not published here until documented.

CuePointe

Community platform

A pool tournament and league concept designed around participation, event management, talent discovery, player records and stronger visibility for the sport.

The opportunity

Pool talent exists across venues and communities, but participation and achievement can be difficult to track consistently across events.

What was designed

  • Player and event records
  • Match and tournament workflows
  • Talent metrics and recognition
  • Finance, sponsor and operations structures

Technology

  • Web platform
  • Structured data workflows
  • Event management
  • Digital records

Evidence

The platform is being developed as a community-driven system. Public outcome metrics will be added as they become verifiable across events.
