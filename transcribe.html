        What to expect

        Close to every word, and honest about the rest.

        On a clear English recording, expect nearly every word to come back right. A phone left far from the speakers, a noisy room or two people talking at once lower that. Voices are told apart as Speaker A, B, C and on, for any number of people; two similar voices, or a one-word reply, are sometimes mixed up.

        • Words the engine was unsure of are underlined, so you check those first.
        • Names and special words typed before you send the file help the engine spell them your way.
        • Every paragraph can be corrected or moved to another speaker, and every speaker renamed. The changes are kept.
        • The recording is deleted as soon as it is transcribed. Only the words are kept, until you delete them.

        Questions

        Before you send a recording.

        Is my recording kept?

        No. It is held in a private store only while it is transcribed, then deleted; anything left over is deleted within a day whatever happens. The words are kept in your account until you delete them, with one button.

        How long does it take?

        Sending takes as long as your connection needs for the file: an hour of audio as an MP3 is about 60 MB. Transcribing an hour usually takes a few minutes. You can close the page once the file is sent; the transcript waits under Your transcripts and in Your history.

        Which languages does it understand?

        English is the strongest, including East African accents. French, Arabic, Spanish, Portuguese and German are recognised too, and the language can be worked out from the recording. Swahili is new: check the result before you share it. Luganda is not supported yet.

        How are the minutes written?

        Press Write the minutes and Claude reads the transcript, with the names you gave the speakers, and writes a summary, the key points, the decisions, the action items with who and by when, and chapters that jump to their place. It uses only what was said: no advice and no invented names, dates or figures.

        What can I download?

        Word and PDF for the transcript and the minutes, plain text, and SRT captions for a video. Copy all puts the whole transcript on your clipboard.
