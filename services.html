POLSSE™ · SpeakPower framework

Pressure-test the story before it travels.

A strong story still has to survive institutions, power, rules, security concerns and real social and economic conditions. POLSSE is the lens SpeakPower uses to test whether a message is ready for the real world.

Who has influence? Consider the power, policy and public environment around the story.

Can people carry the story consistently? Test leadership, teams, partners and frontline interpretation.

What must be defensible? Check claims, promises, disclosures and boundaries.

What happens after it travels? Think about privacy, data, reputation and out-of-context reuse.

Does it fit real life? Test incentives, affordability, social conditions and lived context.
