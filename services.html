Services

Five ways we close the gap between message and impact.

Every engagement starts with the same question: what do you actually need your audience to believe, and what's stopping that from happening right now? The service depends on the answer.

01
In-person or virtual

Strategy & Market Development

Go-to-market messaging built on data — market research, audience behavior, and competitive positioning — not assumption. We map where your message actually needs to compete, and where it doesn't.

  • Positioning built from real market and audience data
  • Go-to-market messaging architecture
  • Competitive and category analysis
02
Virtual — one session

Communication Audits

Face the struggle of a lackluster presentation or a bloated report head-on. We rework bland slides and jargon-heavy documents so your audience stays engaged, informed, and clear on what to do next.

  • Deck and document review, line by line
  • A structural diagnosis: what's true, what's clear, what survives repetition
  • A rebuilt draft, not just a list of notes
03
In-person coaching or live virtual

Public Speaking Through Data

Transform nerves into confidence — not through generic delivery drills, but by structuring your talk around the evidence your specific audience needs to hear. Confidence follows clarity, not the other way around.

  • Pitch and talk restructuring around real evidence
  • Rehearsal under realistic pressure, not a quiet room
  • Delivery coaching tied to your actual content, not generic technique
04
In-person or virtual, team-based

Team Workshops

Confront fragmented team messaging and turn it into cohesive storytelling. Your team leaves with a structured framework aligned to one brand narrative — turning dissonance into harmony.

  • A single message audit across every team member's version
  • Shared frameworks for sales, support, and leadership
  • Ongoing alignment check-ins, not a one-off workshop
05
Virtual, project-based

Narrative Architecture for Startups & Nonprofits

For founders preparing to raise, and NGO leaders preparing to mobilize a community or a donor base. We rebuild your story around a single, defensible thesis — rooted in local impact, legible to a global audience.

  • One core narrative, tested against real objections
  • Pitch, proposal, and stakeholder-story alignment
  • Built to survive being repeated by someone else, badly
