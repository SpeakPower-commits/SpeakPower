POLSSE™ · SpeakPower framework

Pressure-test the message before it leaves the room.

A message can sound perfect in the meeting and still fail once it meets institutions, rules, power, security concerns or the lived reality of the people receiving it. POLSSE helps you test those conditions before they become surprises.

Who has influence? Consider the public, policy and power environment around the message — who may repeat it, challenge it or interpret it differently.

Can the organization carry one meaning? Check whether leaders, teams, partners and frontline staff can repeat the message consistently.

What must be true and defensible? Identify claims, promises, disclosures and ethical boundaries that shape what you can safely say.

What happens after it travels? Consider privacy, data, reputation and the risk created when a message is copied, forwarded or taken out of context.

Does it make sense in real life? Test the message against people's social conditions, economic choices, incentives and lived context.

Use it before

A launch, pitch, campaign, donor proposal, public statement or major internal change.

Use it when

The same message must travel across different audiences, institutions or channels.

Use it to ask

“What changes when this message leaves the room?”
